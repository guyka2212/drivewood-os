# Drivewood 1.1.6

fastfetch in every new terminal.

## New
- **Opening a terminal shows fastfetch.** You see the Drivewood picture with your system info (PC, CPU, memory, uptime, Drivewood version) each time you open a terminal. In a narrow window, like half the screen, the picture moves above the info so nothing gets cut off. In a very narrow window it shows the info only.

## Good to know
- Don't want it? Delete the two lines under `# ---- Drivewood: fastfetch ----` near the end of `~/.bashrc`. Updates won't add them back.
- As always, your files, apps, Settings choices, your own shortcuts and your Link sign-in all stay.
- No new installer this time: the 1.1.2 installer ISO installs Drivewood, and the built-in updater brings it to 1.1.6.

## Files in this release
- `drivewood-update-1.1.6.tar.gz` (+ `.sha256`): used by the built-in updater.
- `drivewood-link.apk`: DriveWood Link for Android.
