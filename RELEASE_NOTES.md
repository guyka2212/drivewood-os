# Drivewood 1.1.8

Vim comes with Drivewood.

## New
- **Vim is installed.** Type `vim` in a terminal, or open Vim from Apps. nano is still there too.

## Good to know
- As always, your files, apps, Settings choices, your own shortcuts and your Link sign-in all stay.
- No new installer this time: the 1.1.2 installer ISO installs Drivewood, and the built-in updater brings it to 1.1.8 (and installs Vim).

## Files in this release
- `drivewood-update-1.1.8.tar.gz` (+ `.sha256`): used by the built-in updater.
- `drivewood-link.apk`: DriveWood Link for Android.
