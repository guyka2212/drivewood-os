This policy explains what DriveWood Link (the "App") and its account service (the "Service")
collect, why, where it is kept, and your rights. [developer name] ("we") is responsible for this
data (the "controller").

## 1. The short version
- Your files, text, clipboard and screen go **directly between your own devices**. We never receive them.
- We store only what is needed to run your account: username, device list and transfer history (names, sizes, times; never contents).
- No ads, no tracking, no selling or renting your data, no profiling, and no AI training on your data.
- You can see, export and delete everything, at any time.

## 2. What we collect
| Data | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account | username, email (if given), password **hash** (handled by Supabase Auth; we never see your password) | to sign you in |
| Consent record | which Terms and Privacy versions you accepted, and when | to prove you agreed, and to ask again when they change |
| Devices | device name, type (computer or phone), random device id, public encryption key, last-seen time, local network address | to list your devices and connect them securely |
| Transfer history | file names, sizes, sender and receiving device, time, success or failure | so you can see your history |
| Control sessions | which device controlled which, start and end time | for your security log |
| Technical logs | IP address and time of sign-in requests, kept by our hosting provider | security and abuse prevention |

**We do not collect:** file or text contents, clipboard contents, screen images, keystrokes or mouse input, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ids, or analytics.

## 3. What stays on your devices only
Content you send, screen streams during take control, and clipboard data travel **encrypted (TLS) directly between your devices** on your local network. They are never uploaded to us. Android's screen-capture and accessibility permissions are used **only** during a take-control session that you accepted on that phone.

## 4. Legal bases (for GDPR and similar laws)
- **Contract:** account, device list and transfers, to provide the Service you asked for.
- **Legitimate interest:** security logs and abuse prevention, kept to the minimum.
- **Legal obligation:** keeping consent records, and answering lawful requests.
- **Consent:** optional features you turn on yourself (for example, shared clipboard). You can turn them off at any time.

## 5. How long we keep it
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account and consent record | until you delete your account |
| Device entries | until you remove the device or delete your account |
| Transfer and control history | 90 days, or less if you clear it |
| Hosting security logs | up to 30 days |

When you delete your account, your data is deleted **within 30 days**, including from backups when those rotate (at most 30 more days). We keep only what the law requires, such as a record of a deletion request, and only for as long as it requires.

## 6. Who processes it
- **Supabase** (database and sign-in) hosts the account data in [Supabase region], under its data processing agreement.
- No one else. We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.
- We disclose data only if a valid, legally binding request requires it. Where the law allows, we tell you first and challenge requests that are too broad. Since we never hold your file contents, we cannot hand them over.

## 7. International transfers
If your data is stored outside your country, we rely on the legal safeguards required for that transfer (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Supabase's agreement).

## 8. Security
- Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth.
- Database rows are protected so each user can read only their own data (row-level security).
- Device-to-device traffic is encrypted. Devices from other accounts cannot connect, and each control session must be accepted on the controlled device.
- If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires (within 72 hours where GDPR applies).

## 9. Your rights
Wherever you live, you can:
- **see and export** your data (App → Settings → Account → Export, as a JSON file);
- **correct** it;
- **delete** your account and data (App → Settings → Account → Delete account);
- **object** to or **restrict** processing, and **withdraw consent** for optional features;
- **complain** to your data protection authority.

Requests by email to [contact email] are answered within 30 days, free of charge.

## 10. Children
The Service is not meant for children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA and UK) without a parent's agreement. If we learn we have data from a child without that agreement, we delete it.

## 11. Open source
When the App's source code is published, anyone can check what it collects. This policy covers **our** Service. A modified version that uses a different backend is run by whoever operates it, under their own policy.

## 12. Changes
We will tell you in the App **at least 30 days before** a change that collects more data or uses it in a new way, and ask you to accept again. We will never start using existing data for a new purpose without your consent.
